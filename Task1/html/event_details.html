Details of Important Events During the Gaza War

Al-Ahli Arab Hospital Bombing

On October 17, 2023, an airstrike targeted the Al-Ahli Arab Hospital in Gaza City, resulting in the deaths of hundreds of civilians. The hospital was sheltering injured patients and displaced families.

  • Estimated 500+ casualties
  • Hospital run by Anglican Church
  • International condemnation followed

Tent Camp Bombing in Nuseirat Refugee Camp

In June 2024, an Israeli airstrike struck tents housing displaced families in the Nuseirat Refugee Camp. Many were killed in their sleep. The tragedy highlighted the vulnerability of those fleeing other attacks.

  • Dozens killed including women and children
  • Tents offered no real protection
  • Witnesses described horrific scenes

Bombings of Schools in Gaza

Throughout the war, several schools used as shelters or educational facilities were bombed. These attacks endangered students and displaced civilians alike.

  • UN schools were not spared
  • Children were killed inside classrooms
  • Some schools bombed multiple times

Global Inaction Amid Gaza Famine

Despite growing hunger in Gaza, international aid has been limited and inconsistent. Children suffer from malnutrition, while world leaders fail to intervene effectively.

  • Babies dying from hunger
  • Humanitarian convoys blocked
  • UN warns of famine, but aid remains minimal
